§ 01 why we built this

A tool that does almost nothing.

Most photo apps want to be everything. Filters, stickers, AI skies, AI bodies, AI memories. They want to make your photos into something else.

Then & Now does almost nothing. You pick two to four photos from your camera roll. You put them next to each other. You drag a slider to compare. If you want the app to straighten the horizon and line up the subject, you tap a button. If you'd rather do it yourself, you do it yourself.

We made this because the before-and-after moment — the fitness check at month three, the renovation reveal, the haircut that finally grew out — is one of the few genuinely private stories a phone holds. We didn't want to be the company that quietly uploaded it to a model-training set. So we built an app that cannot do that. There is no upload. There is no server. There is no account. There is no analytics SDK. There is only a folder on your phone that holds your photos.

That's the whole pitch. If it sounds like a tool that respects you, it's because that's the only kind of tool we wanted to make.

— chengnan & the Then & Now team

§ 03 six features

Things other apps don't bother doing.

Six features. No social feed. No AI face-blur. No "AI enhance". Just the small handful of things every comparison needs to look right.

Auto-straighten, in batch.

Pull four photos of the same gym wall taken months apart and the app detects the floor line in each. All four rotate to match — in a single tap.

preview · 4 cells, auto-rotated

Manual first. Auto second.

Every cell opens exactly as you shot it — tilt, off-center subject, all of it. No invisible magic. The button labelled "Auto Compare" only runs when you tap it, and it preserves any position you've already set.

invariant #1 · auto is opt-in, never default

Axis-locked drag.

Move a cell in 4-grid and the first direction you commit locks. Vertical-only alignment, horizontal-only nudge — whichever your gesture started with.

Three ways to compare.

Arrange photos side by side, drag to wipe between them, or blend them with an overlay. No per-template paywall, no surprises.

Stamp. Just type anything.

No "X MONTHS" presets. No OCR of the EXIF. You tap the stamp slot and type whatever you want — emoji, date, quote, swear word. The app's job is to render what you wrote, not to decide what you meant.

invariant #11 · stamp is fully manual, no smart defaults

Export to PNG.

Export a clean 1:1 image. Save to Photos, share via AirDrop, post anywhere — the file is yours, with no watermark.

Export to MP4.

A timelapse video of your cells animating from oldest to newest. Burned-in labels, on-device encoding, no upload step.

§ 05 privacy

Five iron rules. No exceptions.

Privacy in Then & Now isn't a setting. It's an architecture. There are five rules that the codebase enforces, and we won't ship a build that breaks any of them.

Rule 01

No developer-run server.

Your photos are never uploaded, and there is no analytics SDK, Firebase, or crash reporter. The app communicates with the App Store through Apple StoreKit only to retrieve Pro product information and purchase entitlements; payment details are handled by Apple.

Rule 02

No iCloud sync.

Your comparisons live in Documents/Photos/ on the device. There is no iCloud Key-Value, no CloudKit container, no SyncEngine. Deleting the app deletes every byte.

Rule 03

No analytics.

No event tracking, no screen-time metric, no A/B test. We don't know which features you use. Apple's required PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares zero collected data types and only the UserDefaults API reason (CA92.1).

Rule 04

No account.

There is no Sign in with Apple, no email, no password. Open the app, use it. The only way we know who you are is that you're holding the phone.

Rule 05

All processing on-device.

Vision (subject detection, horizon), Core Image (straighten, crop), AVFoundation (timelapse encoding) — every line runs locally. The phone's GPU does the work, not someone else's server.

§ 08 questions

Things people ask before downloading.

Is Then & Now actually free?
Core comparison features and PNG export are free, with no ads or subscription. Free video export includes 3 exports per month with a Then & Now brand watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited video exports, removes the brand watermark, and enables custom logo upload.
What does auto-align actually do?
Two passes, both on-device. First, Vision detects the horizon in each photo and rotates the cell to match. Second, Vision finds the most salient subject (usually a person or a face) and shifts each cell so the subject's centre sits on the same line. The result is a comparison where the framing is consistent.
What if I've already moved cells by hand?
Auto-align treats manual placement as authoritative. It only nudges cells you've left at default position. If you'd rather start fresh, the toolbar has a Reset button that reverts every cell to default before auto-align runs.
What happens if I delete the app?
Everything. The SwiftData store and the Documents/Photos folder are scoped to the app's container. Uninstall the app and the OS removes the container; there's no server holding a backup because there is no server.
Which iOS versions are supported?
iOS 17 and later. The minimum is set by SwiftData and the modern Vision APIs the auto-align pipeline uses. iPad runs the iPhone build unchanged.
Why no Android?
Two reasons. First, the auto-align pipeline relies heavily on Apple's Vision and AVFoundation stacks, which would need to be rewritten on Android. Second, we want to ship a small, finished thing rather than a sprawling unfinished thing. If Android demand is sustained after launch, we'll revisit.
