05Good to know

A few honest answers.

What is free?

Core comparison features and PNG export are free, with no ads or subscription. Video export includes 3 free exports per month with a Then & Now brand watermark.

What does Pro unlock?

A one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited video exports, removes the brand watermark, and enables custom logo upload. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

Where are my photos stored?

In the app’s local container on your iPhone. There is no developer-run photo backup. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Which devices are supported?

Then & Now requires iOS 17 or later. Version 1.0 is designed for iPhone.

Is an Android version coming?

Not yet. The current focus is a small, finished iPhone app. Android may be considered after launch.
